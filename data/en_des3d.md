## Saki Hayashi

### Background Models

#### DieCutBlocks

![image](data/img/3ddesign/modelerImages_29.jpg)

Even within the specified polygon count, the model placed a relatively heavy load on the system, so I was asked to reduce it.
While preserving the original shape as much as possible, I reduced the polygon count by 1,032.
Since the blocks were intended to be wooden, I also put considerable effort into creating the wood grain texture.

#### Airplane

![image](data/img/3ddesign/modelerImages_30.jpg)

Starting from the initial polygon count, I reduced the model by 331 polygons,
removing areas that had become unnecessary, including the yellow, red, and blue sections.
I also specified details such as the wing parts to give it the appearance of a toy airplane.


#### Spike

![image](data/img/3ddesign/modelerImages_31.jpg)

I was told that using the original single-part model in a connected arrangement created too much of a performance load,
so I created multiple data variations with different connection patterns.
To help convey the intended world and atmosphere, I made the lines of the wings visible
and added extra thickness to the shadows through the shader settings.

### Supporting Character Motion

#### UFO

![image](data/img/3ddesign/modelerImages_49.jpg)

To create the feeling of riding in a UFO,
I focused on keeping it constantly floating and gently bobbing.
During moments of celebration, I incorporated movements unique to a UFO
so that the animation would feel different from that of a normal character.

#### Piyosuke (Duck)

![image](data/img/3ddesign/modelerImages_50.jpg)

I made use of the soft, bouncy characteristics of both the duck and the inflatable ring,
so that not only the duck but also the ring would bounce and sway.
I also paid close attention to the gestures and movements
to bring out as much of the duck's natural cuteness as possible.


#### Penguin & Polar Bear

![image](data/img/3ddesign/modelerImages_51.jpg)

By slightly offsetting the timing of their movements,
I aimed to make each one feel like a separate living creature.
Since their facial expressions do not change,
it was challenging to convey their emotions through movement alone.


#### Horse

![image](data/img/3ddesign/modelerImages_52.jpg)

While making sure the movement did not become too similar to the other characters,
I focused on expressing the horse's strength and characteristic movements as much as possible.
I experimented with the animation to make it feel distinctly horse-like
and expressed it in my own way.


#### Toy Professor

![image](data/img/3ddesign/modelerImages_53.jpg)

I focused on expressing the gentle feeling of the professor while explaining something.
I added gestures and hand movements as though the professor were explaining something while speaking,
so that the sense of giving an explanation would come across as clearly as possible.

#### Gachapon

Since this is a scene where the supporting character is being rescued,
I created the motion while discussing it with the team leader.
In particular, I incorporated small trembling movements and a sudden, energetic opening
to make sure the animation matched the scene.
