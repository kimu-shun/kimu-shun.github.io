## Hina Takizawa

### Game UI

#### Title Logo

![image](data/img/2ddesign/toypair_image_2.jpg)

I focused on making the logo easy to read and understand while also conveying the overall world and atmosphere of the game.
To express the poppy, toy-like world of the game, I made the lettering bold and gave it a rounded, friendly shape.

#### Character Icons

![image](data/img/2ddesign/toypair_image_6.jpg)

The icons that appear in the main game are displayed when a character is rescued.
By giving them a badge-like design, I wanted to create a sense of fun that makes players want to collect them, as well as a sense of accomplishment from obtaining new characters.

![image](data/img/2ddesign/toypair_image_7.jpg)

I created a PC background using the character icons.
I focused on creating an exciting and playful atmosphere.

#### Map Screen

![image](data/img/2ddesign/toypair_image_5.jpg)

Taking advantage of the rescue team setting, I incorporated motifs such as stamps and files.
I created a playful design that allows players to feel as though they themselves have become a member of the rescue team on-site.

![image](data/img/2ddesign/toypair_image_4.jpg)

With the goal of creating a "design that can be understood at a glance,"
I considered visibility and usability while repeatedly experimenting and refining the design.

#### Controls & Transitions

![image](data/img/2ddesign/toypair_image_3.jpg)

Through repeated discussions with the planners and programmers, I developed the designs while sharing ideas about the movement and feel of the controls during implementation, keeping the actual game screen in mind throughout the process.

## Noa Asano

### Game UI

#### Button UI

![image](data/img/2ddesign/artistImage_1.jpg)

Since this UI is used on the title screen, I made it clear and easy to understand, with a crisp, poppy design that would increase the sense of excitement when opening the game.

#### Controller

![image](data/img/2ddesign/artistImage_2.jpg)

Since this UI is used to explain the tutorial, I created a simple design that is clearly recognizable as a Nintendo Switch controller.
In contrast to the controller, I gave the background a cute design so that it would maintain the overall world and atmosphere of the game.

![image](data/img/2ddesign/artistImage_3.jpg)

I kept the design consistent with the controller background, focusing on unity and visibility when creating it.

#### Results

![image](data/img/2ddesign/artistImage_4.jpg)

Since this is the results screen for the rescue team's investigation, I designed it to look like a report.
For the medals and bond levels, I wanted to create elements that would give players a sense of accomplishment, so I designed them to become more elaborate and luxurious as the player progresses to higher levels.

![image](data/img/2ddesign/artistImage_5.jpg)

Although this is a game where two players cooperate,
we discussed with the planner how to make each player's individual score visible and easier to understand.

#### Player Selection

![image](data/img/2ddesign/artistImage_6.jpg)

Although this is a small UI element, I prioritized readability while also giving it a poppy design with a toy-like feel.

#### Trend Mark

![image](data/img/2ddesign/artistImage_7.jpg)

I refined the logo created by another designer and adjusted its colors to match those used throughout the game.
