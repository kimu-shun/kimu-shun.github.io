### Switch用のビルド

#### Cross-Platform Wrapper Development

Faced with the constraint of having only one physical SDK license available for a multi-developer team, I engineered a workaround to maintain team productivity. I integrated the JoyconLib library for Windows and developed a custom wrapper. This allowed the team to develop and test seamlessly on Windows while ensuring the codebase remained fully compatible for deployment on the Nintendo Switch.

#### Mentoring

I joined the project as a technical mentor, responsible for code reviews and guiding junior programmers on best practices. I also supervised the final build process, ensuring that optimization standards and performance best practices were strictly followed to guarantee smooth gameplay on Nintendo Switch hardware.
